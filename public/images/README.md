# 📸 Finu & Kaakooka Photo Gallery Guide

Welcome! Here is where your real photographs live.

## Important Note About Our Story:
- **2nd to 9th Standard**: No real photographs exist from these childhood years. The website uses warm illustrations, scrapbook elements, and nostalgic storytelling for these early memories.
- **From 10th Standard Onwards**: Real photographs begin!

---

## 📁 Folder Structure for Adding Your Real Photos:

```text
public/images/
├── school/
│   ├── class-10/           <-- Photos from 10th standard at MMET
│   ├── class-11/           <-- Photos from 11th standard at DUHSS Panakkad
│   └── class-12/           <-- Photos from 12th standard at DUHSS Panakkad
├── college/                <-- Photos from Farook College & Govt College days
├── friends-weddings/       <-- Photos taken together at friends' weddings
└── other-memories/         <-- Any other special milestones & candid moments
```

### Recommended File Names:
- `school/class-10/photo-01.jpg`, `school/class-10/photo-02.jpg`
- `school/class-11/photo-01.jpg`
- `school/class-12/photo-01.jpg`
- `college/photo-01.jpg`, `college/photo-02.jpg`
- `friends-weddings/wedding-01.jpg`, `friends-weddings/wedding-02.jpg`
- `other-memories/memory-01.jpg`

You can also edit captions and image paths anytime in:
`src/data/anniversaryContent.ts`
