🎶 BACKGROUND MUSIC — HOW TO ADD YOUR SONG
========================================================================

Hi Nasim!
To add your favorite romantic song:

1. Drop an MP3 file into this folder named:
   our-song.mp3

2. The website will automatically detect and play it when Finu clicks
   the "Play Our Music" button in the bottom-left corner!

Note: If no mp3 is present, the website automatically plays a built-in
soothing music box lullaby chime so the romantic music player always
works out of the box!
